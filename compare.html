Every engine's shortcuts, in boxes you tick

Google has an advanced search page and DuckDuckGo has a help page of search syntax. Refine puts the shortcuts for all three engines in one app, as boxes you tick.

What Refine does not do

Refine is not a search engine and has no results of its own: it writes the search and your browser shows the engine's results, adverts included. Engines can drop a shortcut at any time; Refine marks the ones that may no longer work. It is an Android app only.
