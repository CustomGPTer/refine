Every shortcut and filter

This is the list Refine works from, as of version 1.0. “Sent in the link” filters cannot be typed in the search box: they live in the web address, which is why an app helps. Anything written with an equals sign, such as tbs=li:1, is one of these. “Sometimes” means the search engine has not switched the shortcut off, but it does not always do what it says.

The everyday ones

ShortcutWhat it doesWorks on
wordsSearch for. The subject you are looking for. Pages should mention all of these words.
Example: confined space rescue plan		Google: yes
Bing: yes
DuckDuckGo: yes
"exact phrase"Exact phrase. The words must appear together, in this order. Put a * inside to stand for any missing word, as in "safe * of work".
Example: "permit to work"		Google: yes
Bing: yes
DuckDuckGo: yes
(this OR that)Any of these. A page only needs one of these. Good for words that mean the same thing.
Example: (template OR form OR checklist)		Google: yes
Bing: yes
DuckDuckGo: sometimes
-wordLeave out. Pages that mention any of these are dropped. Works with a single word or a phrase. DuckDuckGo shows fewer of these pages but may not remove them all.
Example: -training -"for sale"		Google: yes
Bing: yes
DuckDuckGo: sometimes
filetype:pdfFile type. Only files of this kind. Tick more than one and a result can be any of them. ext: does the same job. DuckDuckGo only knows PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and web pages.
Example: (filetype:docx OR filetype:pdf)		Google: yes
Bing: yes
DuckDuckGo: yes
site:example.comOnly from these sites. Only results from this website. You can give a whole site, one part of it (hse.gov.uk/pubns) or an ending (.gov.uk). Tick a site group to use a saved list in one go.
Example: site:hse.gov.uk		Google: yes
Bing: yes
DuckDuckGo: yes
-site:example.comNever from these sites. Results from these websites are dropped. Tick a site group to leave out a saved list in one go.
Example: -site:pinterest.com		Google: yes
Bing: yes
DuckDuckGo: yes
after:2024-01-01 before:2024-12-31Date. Only pages from this period. Pick a quick button, or set a From and a To date. Either end can be left open. Search engines go by when they think a page was published, so this is good but not perfect. The quick buttons use the engine's own date filter. Bing and DuckDuckGo have no typed date shortcut, so their date filter is sent in the link.
Example: after:2024-01-01		Google: yes
Bing: sometimes
DuckDuckGo: yes

Where the words appear

ShortcutWhat it doesWorks on
allintitle: allintext: allinurl: allinanchor:The search words must be in. Says where on the page your Search for words have to be. Title is the heading shown in the results. Page text is the body. Web address is the link itself. Refine writes this as intitle: before each word, which does the same and mixes safely with the other boxes.
Example: allintitle: scaffold inspection checklist		Google: yes
Bing: yes
DuckDuckGo: yes
intitle:wordAlso in the title. This word must be in the page's title, whatever else is on the page.
Example: intitle:checklist		Google: yes
Bing: yes
DuckDuckGo: yes
intext:wordAlso in the page text. This word must be in the body of the page, not just in the title or in links pointing at it. Bing calls this inbody: and Refine swaps it for you.
Example: intext:asbestos		Google: yes
Bing: yes
DuckDuckGo: no
inurl:wordAlso in the web address. This word must be in the page's link. Handy for finding a section of a site, such as inurl:downloads.
Example: inurl:downloads		Google: sometimes
Bing: no
DuckDuckGo: yes
inanchor:wordIn links pointing to the page. Other pages link to this one using this word as the clickable text.
Example: inanchor:"method statement"		Google: sometimes
Bing: yes
DuckDuckGo: no

Example: udm=14		Google: yes
Bing: yes
DuckDuckGo: yes

DuckDuckGo: no

Example: filter=0		Google: yes

Example: pws=0		Google: sometimes

DuckDuckGo: no

Switched off by Google (do not bother)

Google has switched these off. Refine leaves them out, and they are listed here so that you know not to bother with them.

  • cache: Showed Google's saved copy of a page. Switched off in 2024.
  • link: Listed pages linking to a site. Switched off in 2017.
  • info: and id: Showed what Google knew about a page. Switched off in 2017.
  • +word Forced an exact word. Use "quotes" instead.
  • ~word Included similar words. Google now does this by itself.
  • daterange: Dates as Julian day numbers. Use before: and after:.
  • phonebook: Looked up phone numbers. Switched off in 2010.
  • inpostauthor: and inposttitle: Searched Google Blog Search, which has closed.

Let the app type them

Refine has a box for every one of these, and writes the search for you.
