Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026. This policy covers the Refine app for Android (package uk.jj.refine) and this website. Refine is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • Refine builds a search and hands it to the search engine you chose. The developer never sees what you search for.
  • Refine has no accounts and no servers of its own. The developer receives nothing from your phone.
  • There is no advertising and no analytics in the app.
  • Your presets, site groups and search history are kept on your phone and nowhere else.
  • The search engine you send a search to sees that search, as it would if you had typed it yourself. The detail is below.

What stays on your phone

Your search history, presets, site groups and settings are kept in the app's private storage, in one small file that other apps cannot read. Text that you send to Refine with Share, or from the highlight-text menu, is put in the search box and goes no further until you tap Search.

A backup file is made only when you ask for one, and is saved where you choose. It holds your presets, site groups, history and settings in plain text, so treat it as you would the history itself. The cheat sheet PDF holds nothing about you.

What the app can reach

Refine does not ask for your files, your location, your contacts, your camera or your microphone.

These services also receive your device's internet address, as any online service does. Each search engine handles your search under its own privacy policy: Google, Microsoft, for Bing, and DuckDuckGo. The developer of Refine receives none of this.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • History stays until you delete a search from the History tab or use Clear all in Settings. The list holds at most 500 searches, and the oldest drop off.
  • Presets and site groups stay until you delete them.
  • If you use Inside Refine, cookies and saved pages from the search engine are kept in the app's storage. Clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings removes them.
  • Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Refine deletes everything it holds. Backup files and PDFs you saved stay where you put them.
  • Refine asks Android not to include its data in your phone's own cloud backup.

Your rights

UK and EU data protection law gives you rights over personal data that an organisation holds about you, including the right to see it, correct it and have it deleted.

The developer of Refine holds no personal data about you: there is no account, no server and no log of who uses the app. Your history is on your own phone, where you control it directly using the steps above. For searches sent to a search engine, your rights are exercised with that search engine. If you need help, use the contact link below. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Security

The app's own data is held in private storage that other apps cannot read. The app's connection to GitHub uses HTTPS, and so do the search links it builds.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other websites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub, as the host, receives the internet address of visitors and handles it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The app file is downloaded from GitHub as well, on the same terms.

Children

Refine is a general-purpose search tool. It is not directed at children and its developer does not knowingly collect information from anyone. SafeSearch in Refine is a request to the search engine, not a guarantee of what it shows.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed.

Contact

For a question about this policy or about your data, open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please do not include personal details in one.
