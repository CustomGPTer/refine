Refine 1.0 for Android. First release.

Refine builds precise searches for Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo. You tick what you want, such as a file type, a website or a date range, and it writes the search shortcuts for you.

**Added**
- A box for every search shortcut that still does something: exact phrases, any-of words, words to leave out, 34 file types, websites to search in or leave out, and dates by quick button or calendar.
- Where the words appear, the kind of result (plain links, Images, News, Videos, Forums, Books, Scholar, Maps and more), language, country, SafeSearch and exactly-what-I-typed.
- Image, video and book filters, plus the rarer shortcuts: words near each other, number ranges and quick lookups.
- Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo, with a "May not work" label on every hit-and-miss shortcut.
- The finished search shown as you build it, editable by hand, with Copy. Paste a search to have the boxes ticked for you.
- Site groups, presets (22 ready-made), history, a home-screen widget and icon shortcuts.
- Share and the highlight-text menu send text into Refine.
- A cheat sheet of every shortcut, a backup file, and wallpaper colours in light or dark.

Full notes: https://customgpter.github.io/refine/releases.html

Android 11 or later. Download `Refine.apk` below and open it to install.
